The Problem

Infrastructure data should work as hard as the infrastructure itself.

Large developments and infrastructure operators rely on GIS layers, drawings, PDFs, spreadsheets, photographs, videos and inspection records. When that information sits across separate systems, inboxes and individual devices, teams lose time finding what they need.

A Single Source of Truth INTAKTIS keeps mapped assets, documents and work records together in a shared system, so each team works from the same information.
The Platform

Bring mapped assets, information, and operational work together.

01 / Data Hub

Create, import and organize mapped assets.

Create, import, edit, and organize infrastructure assets as points, lines, and polygons across 2D and 3D maps. Calculate lengths and areas, add tags and color coding, and structure assets into clear hierarchies.

02 / Any Data Type

Attach supporting information to each asset.

Attach photographs, videos, PDFs, websites, raster maps, and other information directly to the assets and locations they describe.

03 / Tasking

Assign, track and close out work in place.

Create tasks against specific assets, assign responsibility, set deadlines, schedule recurring work, share progress updates, and attach supporting files as evidence of completion.

04 / Asset History

Keep a structured operational history.

Keep earlier file and map versions available for reference, compare changes over time, and build a structured operational history for each asset.

Use Cases

Built for how different teams operate.

Physical assets are spread across sites, while drawings, photographs, inspection records, and maintenance tasks often live in separate systems. INTAKTIS links that information to the relevant asset and location on the map.

Telecommunications

Coordinate Work Across Dispersed Network Assets

Bring towers, network assets, inspection records, and assigned work into one mapped view. Teams can record site findings, coordinate follow-up, and retain a history for each asset.

Energy and Utilities

Connect Asset Records, Inspections and Maintenance

Link field evidence and technical records to distributed infrastructure assets. Assign inspections and maintenance work from the same system.

Roads and Transportation

Build a Clearer Record of Conditions and Repairs

Map road assets and connect each location to condition records, photographs, and repair tasks. Teams can prioritize issues and retain a record of completed work.

Golf and Resort Operations

Coordinate Maintenance Across Complex Sites

Link maintenance tasks, progress updates, and completion evidence to course, landscape, and facility assets. Managers can review open, overdue, and completed work by location.

Agriculture and Irrigation

Connect Field Evidence to Irrigation Assets

Map irrigation assets and keep field observations, photos, and maintenance tasks tied to the relevant location. Teams can record issues and coordinate follow-up across the site.

Forestry and Conservation

Maintain Location-Based Records Across Large Areas

Map assets and areas of interest, then attach field observations, incident records, and supporting evidence. Updated records make it easier to review changes and coordinate work across large areas.
